Paste into Gmail

  1. Click the copy button for the right person.
  2. In their Gmail: gear icon → See all settings → General → scroll to Signature → + Create new.
  3. Click into the signature box and press Cmd‑V. Not Cmd‑Shift‑V.
  4. Set both dropdowns underneath (new emails / reply‑forward).
  5. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes.
  6. Send a test and tap the phone number to confirm it dials the right person.
